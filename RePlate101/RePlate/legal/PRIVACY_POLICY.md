## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash
  by our authentication provider), and your account type (customer or restaurant).
- **Profile information:** phone number, profile photo, and (for restaurants) business
  name, description, address, cuisine types, business phone/email, and logo/cover images.
- **Listings (restaurants):** food item titles, descriptions, categories, prices,
  quantities, pickup windows, dietary information, and photos.
- **Orders:** items ordered, quantity, total amount, pickup codes, pickup times, and
  order status.
- **Messages:** the content of messages you send to restaurants or customers through the
  App.
- **Payment information:** when you pay for an order, payment card details are collected
  and processed **directly by our payment processor (Stripe)**. We do **not** store your
  full card number; we may store limited, non-sensitive details such as the card brand
  and last four digits, and a payment reference ID.
- **Support and reports:** information you provide when you contact support or report a
  listing, user, or order.
- **Consent records:** the date/time you accepted our Terms of Service and this Privacy
  Policy.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location information:** with your permission, we use your device location to show
  nearby surplus food and calculate distances. You can disable location access in your
  device settings (some features may not work without it).
- **Device and usage data:** device type, operating system version, app version, and
  basic usage/diagnostic information used to operate and improve the App.
- **Identifiers:** a unique account identifier and, if you enable them, push notification
  tokens.

### 3.3 Information we do NOT intentionally collect
- We do not knowingly collect precise financial account numbers, government IDs (unless
  required for restaurant verification, in which case it is disclosed at that time), or
  data from children (see Section 11).

## 4. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Operate the core service: show listings, process orders, generate and verify pickup
  codes, and enable messaging between customers and restaurants.
- Process payments and prevent payment fraud (via Stripe).
- Send transactional communications (order confirmations, pickup reminders, status
  updates) and, with your consent, other notifications.
- Provide customer support and respond to reports.
- Protect the safety and integrity of the App: detect, prevent, and address fraud,
  abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
- Analyze and improve the App's performance, features, and user experience.

## 7. Payments

Payments are processed by **Stripe**. When you enter card details, they are transmitted
directly to Stripe under their security standards (PCI-DSS). We never receive or store
your full card number, CVC, or expiry. We store only a payment reference and limited
display details (card brand and last four digits) to show your saved payment methods and
reconcile orders.

## 8. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide
the App, comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax and transaction records), resolve
disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or
anonymize it. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data (see
Section 10).

## 9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including
encryption in transit, hashed passwords, database access controls, and row-level security
rules that restrict data access to authorized users. No method of transmission or storage
is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password
confidential and notify us of any suspected unauthorized access.
